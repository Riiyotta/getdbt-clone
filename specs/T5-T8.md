**A-3b Events and coalesce-on-demand: single-value dropdown plus search.**

- Opens directly under the control (y +40), 398 wide.
- White background, **2px #632ff5 border, radius 4**, no shadow.

- Option counts: events **3** (4, 4, 13ch); coalesce **4** (13ch each).

Filtering:
- **events**: client-side and instant (under 50ms). The URL becomes **`?filter=<slug>`**. Card count went 14 → 4 → 8 for two different regions. Picking another option replaces the filter rather than adding to it.
- **coalesce**: server re-fetch. The grid is replaced immediately by a **skeleton of 3 pulse cards** (§A-6g). About 1–1.5s later the results arrive, and the URL becomes `?filter=<slug>`. Card count stays 21 per page.

Search:
- Placeholder 6ch.
- **events**: client-side filter on keystroke.
- **coalesce**: debounced server search. It shows the skeleton, then writes **`?query=<q>`** to the URL.
