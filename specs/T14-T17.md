## C. Not measurable / ambiguous / blocked

1. **T16 chrome contradicts PAGES.md and IA.md.** There is no announcement bar and the footer is not the global one (§0, §T16-F). Confirm with the IA owner before wiring the layout.
2. **T15 IA names** `dark-quote-slider` and `story-grid` do not match the live page: the quote is single and static, and the stories are a slider. The closing CTA is half orange / half white, not dark.
3. **T14 has two FAQ sections**; IA lists one.
4. **T17 header block** (H1 plus p) is missing from IA. The mobile filter drawer (375) and the selected-facet chip styling were not opened or measured. The 768 widget layout has an empty gap (widget bug, do not copy). The detail view is widget-internal and only roughly measured.
5. **Pricing mobile slider** speed was not timed (Splide default assumed from T15). The tooltip panel's exact anchor side was measured once (above the icon, centred). The flip behaviour near the viewport edge was not tested.
6. **Summit mobile menu panel** bounds come from the screenshot plus link positions (x115→375, y50→~275 at 375); the panel element itself was not measured directly.
7. **Bugrino** is a commercial display face. The width match is good, but no open face reproduces its squared details. Schibsted Grotesk 500 is the recommendation; Chivo 500 is the fallback.
8. Champions hero parallax: the scale curve is fitted from 6 samples (easeOutQuad over ~700px). The static translateY offsets (86.28 and 104.29) did not change with scroll in the samples.
9. T15 member-portrait and hero-photo chamfers are baked into the images. The clone needs CSS clip-paths to imitate them (corner cut sizes from the screenshot only).
